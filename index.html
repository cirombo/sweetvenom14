what is this place??

Stardust BBS is a simple image board for anime, manga, and internet culture. There are boards for everything from seasonal anime and cosplay to old web nostalgia and fan art. No account needed — just pick a board and start posting!

New here? Check the Rules before posting, and skim the FAQ if you want the full rundown of how things work around here.
